Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

SpriteSwap is a fan-made Fortnite Sprite trading community. We collect only the information needed to operate the site, such as account details, profile information, trade listings, messages, collection status, and moderation reports.

What we collect

  • Email address and username when you create an account.
  • Discord user ID, display name, username, and avatar when you choose Discord sign-in.
  • Profile details you choose to share publicly.
  • Trade posts, ratings, inventory selections, private messages, and reports.
  • Basic technical logs needed for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it

  • To run the marketplace/community features.
  • To help users discover trades and public inventories.
  • To moderate abuse, fraud, spam, or suspicious behavior.
  • To improve the site experience and keep the service secure.

Public information

Your username, ratings, public inventory, and trade listings may be visible to other users. Private messages are intended to be visible only to the participants and authorized moderators when necessary for abuse review.

Third-party services

SpriteSwap uses Discord for sign-in, Supabase for authentication/database hosting, and Vercel for site hosting. We share only the information needed with those providers to operate login, storage, and hosting. We do not sell personal data.

Your choices

You can request deletion of your account or ask questions about privacy by contacting the site operator through the contact method published on the website.

Children

Do not use SpriteSwap if you are not allowed to use online services under the rules in your region or if you do not have permission from a parent/guardian where required.
