SpriteSwap V20 — Trade Board + Site QA
======================================

What changed
- Trade board redesigned to use two comfortable desktop columns instead of tiny 3-column cards.
- Trade Sprite previews are larger and show up to four items per side before a +N tile.
- Your own listing now says YOUR TRADE / YOUR LISTING instead of asking you to sign in for matching.
- New trade-board filters: best matches, offers you need, trades where you own what they want, and your own listings.
- New sorting: smart match order, newest, oldest, and most Sprites offered.
- New trade detail page: trade.html?id=...
- Trade detail pages show exact variants, vault ownership, live age, description, trader rating, actions and share link.
- Notification page now includes live vault-match activity in addition to unread messages.
- Profile/Collection Sprite tiles received another hard override so full artwork stays visible.
- Added keyboard focus styling and polished empty states/responsive layouts.

Setup
- If V19 schema.sql is already installed, NO Supabase SQL changes are needed for V20.
- NO new Vercel environment variables are needed.
- Upload/commit these files at the ROOT of the existing GitHub SpriteSwap repository.
- Vercel should deploy automatically from main.
