SpriteSwap V21
==============

POST TRADE
- Chapter 7 Season 3 is hidden from the Sprite picker.
- Offering side shows only current/new Sprites that you own.
- Wanted side shows only current/new released Sprites.
- Future live-synced Sprites with season "Latest" remain eligible.

PUBLIC ACCESS
Vercel Authentication / Deployment Protection is a Vercel project setting, not website code.
To let everybody open SpriteSwap without a Vercel account:
Vercel -> spriteswap -> Settings -> Deployment Protection -> disable Vercel Authentication / SSO for Production.
Discord remains the SpriteSwap account login after this is disabled.
