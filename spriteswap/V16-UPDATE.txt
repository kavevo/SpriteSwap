SpriteSwap V16 — Pre-release live catalog + trade intelligence
==============================================================

NEW: automatic Fortnite.GG Sprite discovery
- The 153 Sprite/variant records shipped with SpriteSwap remain the safe baseline.
- /api/sprites checks https://fortnite.gg/sprites at most once every 30 minutes when the site is being used.
- New Sprite/variant names that are NOT in the baseline are fetched from their Fortnite.GG detail page and stored in Supabase sprite_catalog.
- New catalog rows are merged into the Sprite Vault, profiles, collections, trade image lookup and datalists without editing sprites-data.js.
- Recently discovered rows show NEW · LIVE SYNC.
- A Vercel cron calls /api/sprites once daily as a fallback. This is compatible with Vercel Hobby's once-per-day cron limit.

NEW: live trade intelligence
- Trade cards show LIVE + relative age (just now, 34s ago, 8m ago, 3h ago, etc.).
- Relative ages refresh in-place every 30 seconds.
- MISSING FROM YOUR VAULT if the trader offers a Sprite you do not own.
- ALREADY OWNED if their offered Sprite is already in your collection.
- YOU HAVE WHAT THEY WANT if you own the requested Sprite.
- BEST MATCH FOR YOU when you need their offer AND own their wanted Sprite.
- Supabase Realtime still updates the board when trades are created/closed.

ONE REQUIRED SETUP STEP
Run the updated schema.sql once in Supabase SQL Editor. V16 adds:
- sprite_catalog
- sprite_sync_state

NO NEW VERCEL ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES are required.
Keep your existing Discord + Supabase variables.

NOTE
Fortnite.GG does not provide SpriteSwap with an official instant-new-Sprite webhook. V16 therefore uses low-frequency public-page checks. During active site use the catalog can detect new entries on the next stale check (about 30 minutes); Vercel Hobby also guarantees a daily fallback check.
