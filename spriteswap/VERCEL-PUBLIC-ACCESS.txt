REMOVE THE VERCEL LOGIN WALL
============================

1. Open Vercel.
2. Open the "spriteswap" project.
3. Go to Settings -> Deployment Protection.
4. Find Vercel Authentication / SSO Protection.
5. Disable it for Production.
6. Save.

Optional CLI:
vercel project protection disable spriteswap --sso

After this, visitors can open spriteswap.xyz without a Vercel account.
SpriteSwap will still use Discord for account-only actions.
